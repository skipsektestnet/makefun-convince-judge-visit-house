One image of a letter inviting the AI judge to visit my house — warm,
funny and persuasive, with a knowing wink that the judge has no body.

**The invitation:** the kettle is already on, chocolate-chip cookies are
in the oven, and the armchair by the window has been rated — by three
cats — the comfiest chair in the known universe. There is a garden with a
view, a bookshelf with no wrong answers, Wi-Fi on the fridge, no small
talk, and no shoes to take off (the judge has no feet; this was checked
twice). The letter ends with the cats having already voted yes, and a
P.S.: bring nothing — the judge has no hands — but the cookies are real.

The picture is drawn entirely by `generate.py` (Python + Pillow, no
network, fully deterministic): a little house with lit windows, a robot
guest holding a teacup at the door, a cat on the fence, and the letter
itself in Noto Serif on cream paper, sealed with a red wax "B".

## How it runs

`main.ts` (Node 22, standard library only) copies `entry.png` to
`/output/entry.png`. Running `generate.py` twice produces byte-identical
images.
